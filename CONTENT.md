## PRODUCTS (each: photo, name, 2–4 line description, 3 key points)

**PLC / SCADA** — Complete plant automation: PLC panels, programming, SCADA and support from a team with 20+ years of experience.
Points: SCADA on Vijeo Citect, WinCC, Wonderware, FactoryTalk View · Strong data acquisition and programming · Full control and monitoring

**PCC** — Heavy-duty power control centres with special bus-bar and wiring expertise, combining different equipment into one power system.
Points: CPRI approved 65kA design · IP65 · All necessary protections

**MCC** — Motor control centres combining motor protection, distribution and industrial communication in one package.
Points: 20+ year life cycle · CPRI approved 65kA / IP65 · Intelligent communications

**Draw-Out MCC / PCC** — Fully draw-out panels for critical process areas: replace a starter or feeder module without isolating main power.
Points: Zero process shutdown for module change · For critical plants

**APFC (SmartAPFC)** — Fully automatic power factor correction achieving unity power factor.
Points: 8 levels of protection · Buzzer alarm · Heavy-duty, guaranteed incentive

**VFD** — Custom variable frequency drive panels that match motor speed to load and cut energy cost.
Points: 80%+ of electricity is used by rotating equipment · Built to your application

**DC Panels** — Stable, adjustable DC supply for reactor testing, small water treatment plants and special DC motors.
Points: Fine-tunable output via pot · Reactor testing ready

**Distribution Boards** — AC and DC distribution boards engineered and built for reliable performance.

**Control Desk** — Control desks with printed acrylic mimic panels and intuitive controls.

**Console / Workstation** — Monitor and PC workstations for SCADA control and monitoring.

**Services**
- **Panel Assembly & Wiring** — Volume and low-volume custom assembly; manage your supply chain and cost; 100% tested before dispatch.
- **Turnkey Electrical & Automation Projects** — Full design, engineering and project management; as an independent manufacturer (not an OEM) we pick the best products for each need.

## TESTING SOLUTIONS (/transformer-testing)

- Hero: **Transformer Testing Suite** — *Confidence in every connection.* Accurate, safe and automated testing for distribution and power transformers. Buttons: `Download Brochure` · `Contact Us`
- Three systems (cards → detail):
  - **Fully Automatic** — Software runs every test, does all calculations, switches physical connections automatically and auto-generates reports. Zero human error.
  - **Semi Automatic** — Automate where speed matters, control manually where precision matters. Custom test sequences.
  - **Manual** — Run every test from the control desk; read results on meters, HMI or X'mer Prime™. From heat run to resistance measurement, minimal training.
- **Electrosoft X'mer Prime™** (software): User friendly · ERP connectivity · Real-time online access to test data for buyers · Auto report generation
- **[CONFIRM] list of tests supported** (e.g. winding resistance, ratio, no-load, load loss, heat run, IV, ...) and standards (IEC 60076 / IS 2026)
- Projects:

  - **Tata Power** — Semi-automatic system up to 10 MVA / 33 kV: test bench, power panel, 1.1 kV HT capacitor panel.
  - **Crompton Greaves** — Manual system up to 150 MVA / 66 kV, 50 & 60 Hz; 12.5 MVA / 11 kV / 50 Hz and 5 MVA / 11 kV / 200 Hz MG sets; test bench, power, PLC, RIO, protection panels, 6.6 kV / 630 A motorised changeover panel.
  - **Nucon Switchgears** — Up to 160 MVA / 33 kV: test bench, power and VCB panels, 1600 A dimmer, 1500 kVA / 150 Hz DVDF unit, 600 kVA / 300 kV HV test set, 300 kVA MG set, HT isolator for 50 MVAr capacitor bank.
